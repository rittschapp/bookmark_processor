Environment variables

AWS_ENCRYPTED_ACCESS_CREDS

The access creds that are encrypted. The decrypted string is expected in the format:

aws_access_key_id=<ACCESS_KEY>:aws_secret_access_key=<SECRET_KEY>

The passphrase for the encryption will be required to decrypt the
creds. Please see your administrator if you require the passphrase.
Do NOT store the passphrase on any media!
